  • Confidence is often mistaken for competence, which is why confident people get promoted faster.

  • Humans are more afraid of social rejection than physical pain.
  • People remember negative experiences more vividly than positive ones because the brain prioritizes survival.
  • We are more likely to follow advice that confirms what we already want to do.
  • Many people stay busy not because they are productive, but because they are avoiding uncomfortable thoughts.
  • Being alone for too long makes people emotionally sensitive, but never being alone makes people emotionally shallow.
